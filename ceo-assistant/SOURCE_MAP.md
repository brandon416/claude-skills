# Source Map

This package is an umbrella adaptation of selected material from `brandon416/claude-skills`.

## c-level-advisor

Reused:
- Cross-functional executive decision framing.
- C-suite perspective selection.
- Tradeoff-oriented synthesis.
- Chief of Staff style prioritization.

Normalized:
- Removed host-specific process language.
- Removed any expectation to reveal private reasoning.
- Made the package independent of older internal manifest paths.

## c-level-agents

Reused:
- CEO, COO, CFO, CMO, CTO, CPO, CRO, and Chief of Staff lenses.
- Select only roles that materially improve the decision.
- Synthesize disagreements rather than stacking role outputs.

Normalized:
- Parallel work is conditional on real isolated worker support and observable routing evidence.
- Sequential execution is the safe default.

## business-operations

Reused:
- SOP design.
- KPI and dashboard thinking.
- Meeting and operating cadence.
- Resource allocation, resilience, and process optimization.

Changed:
- Removed the assumption that the organization must have at least ten employees or multiple departments.
- Added explicit solo-founder and very-small-team behavior.
- Biases toward lightweight checklists, three to five KPIs, automation before hiring, and minimal meeting overhead.

## agents/personas

Reused:
- Solo founder, small-business owner, CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, and Chief of Staff perspectives.
- Founder energy, key-person risk, cash discipline, and prioritization concerns.

Changed:
- Personas are decision lenses, not pretend identities.
- Removed references to any one vendor's tool names.
- No persona can claim a tool, credential, authority, or worker that the host does not expose.

## business-growth

Reused:
- Growth strategy.
- Pricing, retention, partnerships, sales, revenue operations, and scaling considerations.
- Economic validation and measurable goals.

Changed:
- Start with the highest-leverage constraint and one or two bets.
- Do not default to fundraising, M&A, international expansion, or enterprise complexity.
- Prefer a measurable low-cost experiment when uncertainty is high.

## Astral-style orchestration adaptation

This package uses the portable orchestration concepts of Orbit, Constellation, Eclipse, and Morph. It does not claim Astral workers executed unless the current host provides genuine isolated worker contexts and route evidence.
